Why we keep it

  • Small and kind — tap a glass whenever you drink one; that is the whole job.
  • The ring fills as the day goes on, and turns gold when you reach the goal.
  • The streak is the days in a row you both reached the goal — today only counts once you get there.
  • Fourteen bars show the last two weeks, so you can see the shape of your days.
  • Reminders only ever come from this page while it is open. Nothing is sent anywhere.
